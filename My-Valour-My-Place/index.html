My Valour, My Place!

Task 2 ★ Express Your Place
Write a poem, prose or song about a special place in or near school. Turn it into a colourful A4 poster!

📍 Choose Your Special Place

Which place in Valour Primary School or near our school makes you smile, feel calm, or remember a special moment?

👀 Look, Listen, Feel!

Be a place detective. Think about what you see, hear, smell and feel. You can use See–Think–Wonder too!

✨ Word Power

Try a simile: “The leaves danced like tiny green butterflies.” Make sure your description matches your real place.

✍️ Choose How to Express Your Place

Your words should show why this place matters to you. Choose one: poem, prose or song.

Poem writing guide

Use short lines to paint a picture with words. Try repetition, rhyme or personification.

Example (for inspiration only):

In the garden, flowers sway,\nBright butterflies dance all day.\nThe gentle breeze whispers to me,\nThis is where I love to be.

🧠 Writing Buddy: Choose a detail from your senses notes. Show it to the reader instead of simply saying “It is nice.” Your own voice is the most important part!

🌟 Polish Your Writing

Read your writing aloud slowly. Tick each success criterion when you are ready.

🔎 Think more deeply

Can you replace “good”, “nice” or “beautiful” with a more specific description? What do you want the reader to feel when they read your work?

🖨️ Save your poster as a PDF

1. Click Download / Print A4 Poster.
2. In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF).
3. Choose A4, portrait, and enable background graphics if needed.
4. Save the PDF to your computer's Downloads folder.

📚 Submit to your EL teacher

1. Open your assigned activity in SLS.
2. Select Upload / Add Attachment if your teacher has enabled it.
3. Open your Downloads folder and select your poster PDF.
4. Upload and click Submit.
