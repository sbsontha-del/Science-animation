⚡ Electromagnet Summary Activity 7.1 Page 136

How to Increase Strength?

1. Increase number of batteries

Supplies more electrical current through the coil.

2. Increase number of turns of wire

Concentrates more magnetic flux around the iron core.

⚠️ Safety Rules (Page 134)

  • Do NOT connect electromagnet too long (gets hot!).
  • Do NOT touch exposed wires with bare hands.
  • NEVER connect to main electrical supply socket!
Electromagnets are temporary magnets. They lose their magnetism when electricity is switched OFF!
