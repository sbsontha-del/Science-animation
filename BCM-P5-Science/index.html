3 · MAP · WORKSPACE

Create your own mind map

Drag nodes anywhere. Double-click a node to rename it. Select a node and choose a colour. Use Free Draw for sketches, or Smart Draw to turn a rough line, arrow, circle or rectangle into a neat shape. Choose a connector style, enter an optional label, click Draw connector, then click two nodes. Click a connector and use Reverse selected connector to change its direction. Add braces/brackets and drag them beside groups; double-click a bracket to enlarge it.

Pointer mode: move and edit your mind map.
