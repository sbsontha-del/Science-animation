Imagine you are Ruby. The heart pumps you around the body through blood vessels. Where will you travel?

❤️ Heart
At the lungs: Blood gains oxygen and releases carbon dioxide.
At the body parts: Blood delivers oxygen and digested food and picks up carbon dioxide.
The heart: Pumps blood. Blood vessels: Carry blood around the body.

Say it aloud: “Heart, lungs, heart, body parts, heart.”
