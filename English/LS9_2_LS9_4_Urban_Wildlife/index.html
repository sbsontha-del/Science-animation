STAGE 1 • SOURCE DETECTIVE

Can we trust every source?

Before deciding whether urban wildlife are friends or foes, ask: Is the information reliable? Is it current?

Source A — Research paper

Wildlife in Singapore, Journal of Wildlife Studies. Written by scientists Sabrina Tan, Chin Seng Neo and Gretel Jabbar. Published one year ago.

Who wrote it? What expertise might they have? How recently was it published? Can the information be checked? Would you trust it? Why?
Source B — Social media post

Information from an unknown source, shared two days ago.

It is recent — but does recent automatically mean reliable? Who created the information? Can you verify the claim? What is the difference between current and reliable?
Source C — Wildlife protection book

Written by Professor A. Roshan, a professor studying wildlife, in 1985.

Does the author's expertise make the source reliable? Is information from 1985 current? Could recommendations have changed? Can a source be reliable but not current?
