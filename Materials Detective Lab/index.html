🧠 What is the difference?

STRENGTH

The name of the physical property.

It is a material's ability to be pushed or pulled without breaking or tearing.

STRONG

A word that describes a material.

A strong material does not break or tear easily.

Memory Tip

STRENGTH names the property. STRONG describes the material.

🧠 What is the difference?

FLEXIBILITY

The name of the physical property.

It is a material’s ability to bend without breaking.

Sentence Pattern:

“The property being tested is FLEXIBILITY.”

FLEXIBLE

A word that describes a material.

A flexible material can bend easily without breaking.

Sentence Pattern:

“The rubber is FLEXIBLE because it bends easily without breaking.”

🙋‍♀️ Question Guide

❓ If the question asks:

“What physical property allows the material to bend?”

👉 Answer: Flexibility

❓ If the question asks:

“How would you describe the material?”

👉 Answer: Flexible

❓ If the question asks:

“Why can the bag be rolled up?”

👉 Answer: The bag is flexible and can bend without breaking.

✅ Check your understanding

Memory Tip

FLEXIBILITY = the name of the property

FLEXIBLE = a word that describes the material

Final Summary:

"Rubber has flexibility. Rubber is flexible because it bends easily without breaking."
